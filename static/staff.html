    Document summary

    Upload a PDF (up to 50 pages) or TXT file, or paste its text, for a plain-language, source-grounded summary. This standalone summary is not saved to a case.

    Why it matters: long papers are a hidden source of delay. A source-grounded summary lets staff see what a document is about, and what is missing, before it reaches the bench.

    Undertrial Watch

    Custody cases from the Docket are checked every day against Sec. 479 BNSS. Anyone past the limit gets an alert with a ready release application for a legal-aid lawyer. Uploaded and imported cases appear before synthetic examples.

    Each alert moves through:

    1. Found
    2. Lawyer approves
    3. Filed in court
    4. Heard
    5. Released / denied
