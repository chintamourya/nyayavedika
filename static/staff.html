    Undertrial Watch

    Custody cases from the Docket are checked every day against Sec. 479 BNSS. A possible case for review gets an alert and draft application. A lawyer reviews the draft and may file it; only the court decides whether to release the person. Staff can assign a pending alert to a lawyer they have checked against the official legal-aid roster. Assignments appear in a shared queue visible to every lawyer account and do not send email or SMS.

    Workflow: lawyer review → application filing → court hearing → court order and, if granted, release.

    1. Alert found
    2. Lawyer review
    3. Filed in court
    4. Hearing held
    5. Court outcome
