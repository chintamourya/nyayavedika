How NyayaVedika cuts the backlog

About 5.9 crore cases are pending in Indian courts, about 64 lakh for over ten years, and about 4 lakh people in prison are undertrials (NJDG, NALSA). Each door removes delay at a different point.

Case triageCourt staff rank cases by urgency and Section 479 eligibility, so people held past their limit are flagged first.
Document summarisationLong FIRs, orders and judgments become source-grounded summaries for citizens and staff, and four structured insights for advocates.
Legal aid accessCitizens check free legal aid eligibility with deterministic rules, not AI guesses.
Scheduling efficiencyCause lists and hearing slots are drafted by rule, with the registrar deciding what is published.
Advocate Document StudioMerge, split, paginate, index and OCR paper-books in one place, so papers reach the bench ready to use.
Advocate AI InsightsIssues, facts, background and summary with page references, from one upload, so long papers are understood faster.

These are the mechanisms. Field outcomes are reported only from real pilot use, never from the demo data.
