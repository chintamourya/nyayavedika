Impact on Timely Justice · Legal Backlog

Indian courts hold about 5.9 crore pending cases and roughly 64 lakh have been pending over ten years. Before a bench can decide, advocates must assemble, paginate, index and read very long papers. Delay at the preparation stage becomes delay in court. These two tools shorten that stage. They do not decide anything: the advocate reviews every output.

Document Studio

  • Helps avoid defective filings. Page numbers, bookmarks and a clickable index make a paper-book navigable, so a bench and the registry can find each document without hunting.
  • Faster paper-book assembly. Merge, split into volumes, reorder, rotate and remove pages in one place instead of several desktop tools.
  • Scanned records become searchable text. OCR turns image-only pages into text that can be quoted and searched.
  • Private by design. Files are processed in memory and are not added to court records.

AI Document Insights

  • Faster comprehension of long papers. Issues, Facts, Case Background and Summarization, each point tied to a page and the exact source text.
  • Read once, ask four times. Extraction and OCR are reused, so each extra insight needs no second pass over the file.
  • Advocate stays in control. Inferences are labelled, and every claim can be checked against the original page.
  • Better prepared hearings. Clearer issues and chronologies can help hearings use court time well, which matters where adjournments feed the backlog.

Undertrial applications - Sec. 479 BNSS

People in custody who appear past the Sec. 479 mark. As counsel you decide whether to file the application. The court decides release; the court registry records filing, hearing and the court's order.

  1. NyayaVedika flags the case and shows the arithmetic
  2. You (counsel) verify the facts and decide to file, or record why not
  3. Court registry records the filing and the hearing
  4. The court orders release on bail / bond, or continued detention with reasons
